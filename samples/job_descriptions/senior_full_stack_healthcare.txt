Senior Full Stack Engineer, Patient Experience - Remote (US)

About us
We build a virtual care platform used by hospitals and clinics to connect patients with
care teams. Our engineers ship features that patients and clinicians rely on every day.

The role
As a Senior Full Stack Engineer on the Patient Experience team you will design and build
patient-facing web applications and the APIs behind them, integrating with EHR systems
through FHIR and HL7 interfaces.

What you'll do
- Build accessible, responsive patient and clinician experiences with React, TypeScript and Next.js.
- Design and operate Node.js and Python services and RESTful / GraphQL APIs.
- Integrate with Epic and Cerner using FHIR R4, SMART on FHIR and HL7 v2.
- Protect PHI: implement HIPAA-compliant security controls, OAuth 2.0 / OIDC, RBAC and audit logging.
- Own features end to end, from design through deployment on Google Cloud (GCP) with Kubernetes.
- Improve reliability with automated testing (Jest, Playwright), CI/CD and observability.
- Mentor engineers and collaborate with product, design and clinical stakeholders.

What we're looking for
- 4+ years of professional full stack software engineering experience.
- Strong TypeScript, React and Node.js; working knowledge of Python.
- Hands-on FHIR R4 or HL7 integration experience with EHR vendors.
- Experience building HIPAA-compliant systems handling PHI.
- PostgreSQL and Redis experience; Docker and Terraform.
- Commitment to WCAG accessibility.

Nice to have
- Google Cloud (GCP), GKE or Kubernetes experience.
- Real-time features with WebSockets or WebRTC (telehealth).
- SOC 2 audit experience.
- Experience with generative AI features in healthcare products.
