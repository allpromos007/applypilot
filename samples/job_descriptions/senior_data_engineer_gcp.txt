Senior Data Engineer - Remote (US)

About the role
We are looking for a Senior Data Engineer to design and scale the data platform that
powers analytics and machine learning across the company.

What you'll do
- Design, build and operate batch and streaming data pipelines on Google Cloud (GCP).
- Own our BigQuery data warehouse, including data modeling and performance tuning.
- Build streaming ingestion with Kafka and orchestrate workflows with Airflow.
- Write production-quality Python and SQL, with tests and CI/CD.
- Manage infrastructure as code with Terraform and deploy services on Kubernetes.
- Partner with stakeholders across analytics, product and ML teams.

What we're looking for
- 5+ years of experience in data engineering.
- Expert Python and SQL.
- Hands-on experience with BigQuery, Airflow, Kafka and Spark.
- Experience with Terraform, Docker and Kubernetes.

Nice to have
- dbt experience.
- Exposure to machine learning pipelines or generative AI.
- Experience mentoring engineers.
